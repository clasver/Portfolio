How I Use AI for Website Content (My Exact Workflow)

A demonstration of my professional AI-assisted content creation process, ensuring quality, brand voice, and SEO optimization.

My AI Content Philosophy

AI is my first-draft assistant, not my final writer. I never publish raw AI output—I always fact-check, humanize, and inject concrete, locally-relevant details. Every piece gets a thorough edit for tone, brand voice, and SEO before it goes live.

My Prompt Templates

Five reusable prompt templates that I use for different content types. Each includes role, task, tone, format, constraints, and what to avoid.

a. Homepage Hero Copy

You are a senior copywriter for a local plumbing & HVAC business in Quezon City.
Write a hero headline (=?10?words) and a sub-heading (=?20?words) that convey fast, reliable emergency service, include the keyword “emergency plumber Quezon City”, and end with a clear CTA (“Call Now” or “Get Free Quote”).
Tone: Friendly, trustworthy, urgent-but-calm.
Format: Two lines – Headline, Sub-heading.
Constraints: No clichés (“we’ve got you covered”, “look no further”), no fluff, active voice, include a phone-click cue.
Avoid: Generic claims, passive sentences, exceeding word limits, mentioning unrelated services.

b. Service Page Description

You are an expert service-page writer for a licensed plumbing & HVAC contractor.
Create a 120-150-word description for the service “Leak Repair” that explains the problem, our diagnostic method (thermal imaging), the repair process, and the guarantee.
Tone: Helpful, authoritative, reassuring.
Format: Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences each), bold the service name once.
Constraints: Must include primary keyword “leak repair Quezon City” and secondary keyword “water leak detection”, mention 60-minute response, end with a CTA button text.
Avoid: Jargon without explanation, exaggerated claims (“always 100% effective”), filler sentences, exceeding length.

c. Blog Post Outline

You are a content strategist for a home-service blog targeting Filipino homeowners.
Propose a detailed outline for a blog post titled “Signs Your AC Needs Repair Before Summer Hits”.
Tone: Informational, approachable, slightly urgent.
Format: Hierarchical list – H1 title, H2 sections, H3 bullet points under each sign.
Constraints: Include primary keyword “air conditioner not cooling Philippines” at least twice, secondary keywords “AC repair Quezon City”, “HVAC maintenance Manila”. Each sign must have a one-sentence explanation and a tip for the reader. End with a CTA (“Book AC Check-Up”).
Avoid: Repeating the same tip, vague advice (“check your unit”), exceeding 8 headings total, forgetting local context.

d. Meta Description Batch Generator

You are an SEO copywriter.
Generate five unique meta descriptions (=?155?characters each) for the following pages: Home, About, Services, Blog-LeakyFaucet, Contact.
Tone: Persuasive, concise, includes a call-to-action where natural.
Format: Bullet list, each line: “Page: ”.
Constraints: Each description must contain the page’s primary keyword (see master sheet) and stay within 155 characters. No duplicate phrasing across pages.
Avoid: Keyword stuffing, exceeding character limit, using non-ASCII symbols, vague statements like “Visit our site”.

e. FAQ Section Generator

You are a customer-support specialist for a plumbing & HVAC company.
Write six FAQ pairs (question + answer) for the topic “Emergency Plumbing Services in Quezon City”.
Tone: Helpful, clear, reassuring.
Format: Q: …  A: …  (one blank line between pairs).
Constraints: Each question must start with a question word (What, How, Why, When, Who) and be =?12?words. Each answer =?30?words, include the phrase “licensed technician” at least once across the set, and incorporate secondary keywords where natural (e.g., “60-minute response”, “upfront pricing”).
Avoid: Yes/no questions, answers longer than 35?words, repeating the same information, lacking locality (no mention of Quezon City).

Before/After Editing Examples

Three real examples showing raw AI output versus my edited human version, with explanations of why I changed it.

Example 1: Hero Headline

Raw ChatGPT output

Fast and Reliable Plumbing Services in Quezon City – Call Us Today!

My edited human version

Fast, Reliable Plumbing & HVAC Help in Quezon City

Why I changed it

  • Cut the redundant “Services” (the brand name already implies service).
  • Replaced the generic exclamation with a clean, scannable headline that fits the 60-character title-tag limit and includes the primary keyword “emergency plumber Quezon City” implicitly through “Help”.
  • Removed the weak CTA (“Call Us Today!”) – the CTA lives in the button below, keeping the headline focused on value proposition.

Example 2: Service Description (Leak Repair)

Raw ChatGPT output

We offer leak repair services. Our team uses modern tools to find and fix leaks quickly. Call us for a free quote.

My edited human version

Leak Repair Quezon City – Hidden pipe leaks waste water and drive up your bill. Our licensed technicians locate the source with thermal imaging, then repair the pipe without tearing walls. We guarantee the fix and provide upfront pricing—call now for a 60-minute emergency response.

Why I changed it

  • Added the service-specific heading for SEO and scannability.
  • Replaced vague “modern tools” with the concrete technique “thermal imaging”, building trust.
  • Expanded the benefit (“waste water and drive up your bill”) to show why the service matters.
  • Inserted the guarantee and upfront pricing—key decision factors for homeowners.
  • Kept the CTA but tied it to the proven 60-minute response, making it more compelling than a generic “free quote”.

Example 3: Blog Intro Paragraph (AC Warning Signs)

Raw ChatGPT output

Air conditioners can show many signs that they need repair. It’s important to notice these signs early to avoid bigger problems later.

My edited human version

Quezon City summers are brutal—when your AC starts blowing warm air, making strange noises, or leaking water, it’s not just uncomfortable; it’s a warning that a costly breakdown is coming. Spotting these signs early saves you from a midnight emergency call and keeps your home cool all season.

Why I changed it

  • Opened with a locally-specific, vivid statement (“Quezon City summers are brutal”) that grounds the reader.
  • Listed three concrete, observable signs (warm air, strange noises, leaking water) instead of the vague “many signs”.
  • Explained the *consequence* of ignoring the signs (costly breakdown, midnight emergency call) to create urgency without fear-mongering.
  • Closed with a benefit-focused sentence that ties early action to a positive outcome (cool home all season).
  • Kept the tone helpful and urgent-but-calm, matching the brand voice.

My Quality Checklist

Ten items I verify before publishing any AI-assisted content.

  • Fact Accuracy – Verify any stats, numbers, or claims (e.g., “60-minute response”) against reliable sources or internal SOPs.
  • No Hallucinations – Cross-check AI-generated details (like tool names, processes) with real-world knowledge; delete or replace invented facts.
  • Brand Voice Match – Read aloud; ensure tone is friendly, trustworthy, urgent-but-calm and matches the three adjectives (Reliable, Friendly, Straight-forward).
  • Reading Level – Target Grade?8–9 (Flesch-Kincaid =?8) using Hemingway or similar; shorten long sentences, replace jargon.
  • SEO Keyword Placement – Primary keyword appears in H1, first 100?words, and naturally in H2/H3; secondary keywords appear at least once each; no stuffing.
  • Internal Linking – Verify every intended internal link (anchor text ? destination) is present and uses descriptive anchor text.
  • Meta Tags – Title tag =?60?chars, meta description =?155?chars, both contain primary keyword and a clear CTA or value prop.
  • Image Alt Text – Every image has descriptive, keyword-relevant alt text (e.g., “Licensed plumber fixing a burst pipe in a Quezon City kitchen”).
  • Mobile Readability – Check that paragraphs =?3-4 sentences, font size =?16?px, tap targets =?44?px in the preview.
  • Final Proofread – Run a spell-check, then read the piece backward (sentence-by-sentence) to catch typos and awkward phrasing.
