Case Study

Pearly Curls — Hairstyle Inspiration Platform

A WordPress-powered content site delivering curated hairstyle inspiration for weddings, prom, and formal events. Built with a focus on clean UX, SEO-driven content architecture, and a 4-step selection flow that helps users find the right look and bring clear references to their stylist.

Project Overview

Pearly Curls is a hairstyle inspiration platform with the tagline "Find the hair. Own the moment." The site serves elegant, save-worthy hairstyle ideas across curated categories: Updos & Buns, Bridal, Prom & Dance, Romantic Styles, Hair Length, and Accessories. Features a 4-step selection flow (occasion → shape → hair match → save reference), evergreen topic hubs, and prom mood boards.

Content Architecture & Categories

Organized into six main categories with supporting evergreen hubs and seasonal mood boards:

  • Updos & Buns
  • Bridal
  • Prom & Dance
  • Romantic Styles
  • Hair Length
  • Accessories

Evergreen hubs: Classic Chignons, Half-Up Curls, Wedding Guest Hair, Pearls/Pins/Slides.
Prom mood boards: Worn Down, Curly, 80s Glamour.

UX: 4-Step Selection Flow

Designed a guided journey to help users narrow down styles efficiently:

  1. Occasion — Wedding, prom, formal event, or everyday
  2. Shape — Updo, half-up, worn down, braided, etc.
  3. Hair Match — Filter by length, texture, and face shape
  4. Save Reference — Collect front/side/back views to show your stylist

Technical Implementation

Built on WordPress with performance and compliance in mind:

  • WordPress CMS
  • ExactDN Image CDN
  • SVG Placeholders
  • TCF-Compliant Cookie Consent
  • Pinterest & X Integration

SEO & Content Strategy

Content organized around high-intent search queries for formal hairstyles. Category pages target broad terms (e.g., "bridal updos"), while individual style posts capture long-tail queries (e.g., "low chignon wedding guest hair"). Internal linking connects occasion → shape → specific style pages. Blog archive provides evergreen traffic foundation.

Skills Demonstrated

WordPress Content Strategy SEO UX Design Information Architecture ExactDN CDN Cookie Compliance Pinterest Marketing Responsive Design Accessibility

What I Learned

Building a content-heavy visual site taught me the importance of structured taxonomy from day one — retrofitting categories later is painful. The 4-step flow showed how guided navigation outperforms open-ended browsing for decision-heavy use cases. ExactDN integration demonstrated how image CDN choice directly impacts Core Web Vitals on media-rich sites. Cookie consent compliance (TCF) is non-negotiable for global traffic.
