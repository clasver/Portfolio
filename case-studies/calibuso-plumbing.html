What I Learned

Managing dual-platform builds deepened my understanding of theme-based versus drag-and-drop constraints. SEO implementation required constant cross-checking between WordPress plugins and Wix SEO Wiz. AI workflow taught me the importance of treating AI as a junior writer — always editing for local nuance, brand voice, and factual accuracy. The Notion tracker highlighted the value of breaking a large project into phases with clear deliverables.
